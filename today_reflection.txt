"Dwell on the beauty of life. Watch the stars, and see yourself running with them." — Marcus Aurelius

In this passage, Roman Emperor Marcus Aurelius is pointing to the Stoic concept of *Sympatheia*—the understanding that everything in the cosmos is interconnected and part of a single whole. When we get consumed by daily anxieties and petty frustrations, stepping back to observe the vastness of nature instantly resets our perspective. By placing ourselves mentally within this grander tapestry, we realize that our personal troubles are small and temporary, while our connection to the world around us is enduring.

**Actionable Exercise for Today:** 
Step outside tonight—or take a brief break during the day—look up at the sky for two minutes, and actively remind yourself that you are an integral part of this vast universe, not an isolated observer carrying the weight of the world alone.
