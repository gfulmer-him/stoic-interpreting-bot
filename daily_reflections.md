

## Daily Reflection
**Quote**
> "We suffer more often in imagination than in reality."  
> — Seneca, *Letters from a Stoic*

**Practical Explanation**
Our anxiety usually stems from catastrophic scenarios we construct in our minds rather than the actual events unfolding before us. By focusing on hypothetical disasters, we waste valuable energy on problems that do not currently exist and may never happen. Returning your attention strictly to what is happening right now strips away this self-imposed fear and restores your clarity.

**Actionable Exercise for Today**
Notice the moment you begin to feel anxious or overwhelmed today and perform a quick "Reality Audit." Ask yourself: *"Is this threat happening right now, or am I imagining a future scenario?"* If it is only in your mind, take a breath, label it as an imaginary scenario, and focus entirely on the physical task immediately in front of you.
