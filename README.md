## 🌟 Today's Stoic Reflection (2026-11-28)

"Very little is needed to make a happy life; it is all within yourself, in your way of thinking." — Marcus Aurelius

We often convince ourselves that peace of mind depends on external conditions—more money, better circumstances, or the approval of others. However, Marcus Aurelius reminds us that true contentment isn't imported from the outside world, but generated by how we choose to interpret our daily experience. When you shift your focus from changing things beyond your control to mastering your internal mindset, happiness becomes an accessible daily choice rather than a distant goal.

**Actionable exercise for today:** Identify one minor frustration you encounter today, pause before reacting, and reframe it in your mind as an opportunity to practice patience or resourcefulness.
